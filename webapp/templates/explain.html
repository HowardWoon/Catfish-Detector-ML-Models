Z-Score Calculator Explanation

Our primary fallback signal is a deterministic Behavioral Z-Score Risk Engine. By mapping user inputs to the normal distribution of the dataset, we calculate mathematical deviation from typical genuine behavior.

Z = (X - μ) / σ

If Z > 2.0 (Top 2.5% of population): Suspicious
If Z > 3.0 (Top 0.1% of population): Critical Flag
