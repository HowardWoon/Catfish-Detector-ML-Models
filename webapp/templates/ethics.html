Ethical AI Framework

At CATFISH.AI, we recognize that deploying machine learning models in social environments carries profound ethical responsibilities. Our system is designed not just to detect fraud, but to protect human emotional well-being without compromising personal privacy.

1. Privacy-First Architecture

We do not read private user messages. Our models operate strictly on Behavioral Metadata (e.g., message frequency, session duration, swipe ratios). By analyzing how a user interacts rather than what they say, we preserve total communication privacy while still identifying malicious bot networks and romance scammers.

2. Explainability & Human Oversight

Algorithms should never operate as a "Black Box" when terminating user accounts. Through our integration of SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations), every high-risk flag generated by our ensemble is fully explainable. If a user is flagged, our system explicitly states why (e.g., "Abnormal Swipe Ratio of 0.99"), allowing human moderators to make fair, informed final decisions.

3. Mitigating False Positives

Incorrectly classifying a genuine user as a "Catfish" is harmful to their digital identity. To combat this, we utilize a 6-Model Ensemble System. A single model anomaly is not enough to trigger a ban; our architecture requires a consensus across distinct algorithms (XGBoost, Neural Networks, Random Forests) to ensure robust fairness.

Our Core Mission

"To secure the digital dating ecosystem from emotional and financial fraud, utilizing transparent, behavior-driven machine learning."
