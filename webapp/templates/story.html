The Data Story

To catch a catfish, we must first understand how they mathematically differ from genuine users. Here is the statistical reality hidden within 50,000 dating interactions.

The Imbalance Problem

Catfish make up a very small percentage of total dating app users. In our dataset, only 7.3% of profiles are malicious. This massive class imbalance is why we utilized SMOTE-Tomek in our ML pipeline to generate synthetic minority examples.

KDE Distributions: Genuine vs Catfish

If we map out individual behaviors, the separation becomes clear. Notice how Catfish (Red) peak at extremes, while Genuine profiles (Green) form normal bell curves.

Mann-Whitney U Tests Confirm Statistical Significance

Because the data is non-parametric, we used the Mann-Whitney U test instead of standard t-tests. The p-values for Message Sent Count and Swipe Right Ratio are < 0.001, confirming the observed behavioral gaps are mathematically undeniable.
